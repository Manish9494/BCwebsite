Candle care & safety

Caring for your candle

A few simple habits keep your candle burning evenly and safely. Sculptural pieces are made to be admired as much as lit.

  1. 2–3 hrs

    Make the first burn count

    On the first light, let the melted wax reach the edges. This helps prevent tunnelling.

  2. 5 mm

    Trim the wick

    Trim the wick before each use for a calm, steady flame and less soot.

  3. 4 hrs

    Rest between burns

    Burn for no more than four hours at a time, then let it cool fully before relighting.

  4. Plate

    Use a heat-safe base

    Sculptural and pillar candles can drip. Always burn them on a heat-resistant plate or dish.

  5. Watch

    Never leave a flame unattended

    Keep burning candles in sight and away from children, pets, drafts and anything flammable.

  6. 1 cm

    Know when to stop

    Extinguish your candle when about 1 cm of wax remains at the base.
